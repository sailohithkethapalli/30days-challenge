
Algorithm: Smart Cart Billing System
1. Start.
2. Input purchase amount, customer type, and delivery distance.
3. Check whether inputs are valid; if not, display an error and stop.
4. Calculate the discount rate based on customer type and purchase amount.
5. Calculate discount and amount after discount.
6. Determine delivery charges based on distance.
7. Calculate final amount = amount after discount + delivery charges.
8. Display the bill details and final payable amount.
9. Stop
